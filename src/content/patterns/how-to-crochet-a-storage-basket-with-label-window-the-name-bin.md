In this tutorial, I'll walk you through making a beautiful crochet storage basket with a handy label window. It's perfect for keeping your toys, craft supplies, or miscellaneous household items organized. Don’t worry if you're new to crochet—I’ll guide you step by step.

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - **Worsted weight (Category 4)** — This is a medium-thickness yarn, easy to see your stitches with. 
    - Main Color: Natural (approx. 300 yards)
    - Accent Color: Denim Blue (approx. 50 yards)
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - 5.0 mm (H-8) hook — This size is perfect for working with worsted weight yarn.
- **Tapestry Needle:** 
  - Useful for weaving in ends once you've finished your project.
- **Scissors:** 
  - For cutting your yarn.
- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** 
  - These help keep track of your rounds as you crochet.
- **Plastic Canvas or Felt for Label Window:** 
  - 1 piece, 8 inches x 4 inches — This creates a sturdy area for your labels.
- **Yarn/Fabric for the Label:** 
  - 1 piece, 8 inches x 2 inches — This is where you can write or attach a name or item label.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Ch (chain):** 
  - This creates the little loops that start your work. To make a chain stitch, yarn over (wrap the yarn over your hook) and pull it through the loop on your hook.
- **Sc (single crochet):** 
  - A basic stitch. Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull through so you have two loops on your hook, yarn over again and pull through both loops.
- **Sl st (slip stitch):** 
  - Used for joining rounds. Insert your hook, yarn over and pull through both loops on your hook.
- **St(s) (stitch(es)):** 
  - Refers to single stitches.
- **Inc (increase):** 
  - To increase, simply put two single crochet stitches in the same stitch.
- **Dec (decrease):** 
  - To decrease, crochet two stitches together by inserting your hook into the next two stitches and pulling through to finish with one stitch.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Part 1: Base of the Basket
1. **Ch 5**, then join with a **sl st** to form a ring. 
2. **Round 1 (R1):** Ch 1, *make 10 sc into the ring.* Join with a **sl st** to the first sc. (10 sts)
3. **Round 2 (R2):** Ch 1, *make 2 sc in each of the next stitches around.* Join with a **sl st**. (20 sts)
4. **Round 3 (R3):** Ch 1, *make 1 sc in the next st, then make 2 sc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (30 sts)
5. **Round 4 (R4):** Ch 1, *make 1 sc in the next 2 sts, then make 2 sc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (40 sts)
6. **Round 5 (R5):** Ch 1, *make 1 sc in the next 3 sts, then make 2 sc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (50 sts)
7. **Rounds 6-R8:** Ch 1, *sc in each st around.* Join with a **sl st**. (50 sts) 

### Part 2: Sides of the Basket
8. **Round 9 (R9):** Ch 1, *sc in each st around.* Join with a **sl st**. (50 sts)
9. **Round 10 (R10):** Change to Accent Color. Ch 1, *sc in each st around.* Join with a **sl st**. (50 sts)
10. **Rounds 11-20:** Ch 1, *sc in each st around.* Join with a **sl st**. (50 sts)

### Part 3: Label Window
11. Cut plastic canvas or felt into an 8-inch by 4-inch rectangle.
12. Attach it to the front side of the basket before closing the top edge. You can pin it for extra security.

### Part 4: Finishing Edge
13. **Round 21 (R21):** Ch 1, *sc in each of the next 4 sts, then do a dec; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (40 sts)
14. **Round 22 (R22):** Ch 1, *sc in each of the next 3 sts, then do a dec; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (30 sts)
15. **Round 23 (R23):** Ch 1, *sc in each of the next 2 sts, then do a dec; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (20 sts)
16. **Round 24 (R24):** Ch 1, *sc in the next st, then do a dec; repeat from * around. Join with a **sl st**. (10 sts)

Fasten off and weave in ends. **Beginner Tip:** It’s okay if your stitches aren’t perfect! Just take your time.

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
- Use a ***ladder stitch***—a method of sewing—to attach the label piece onto the plastic canvas window if desired.
- Ensure that the label area is open for inserting a name card or label. 
- Secure the plastic canvas to the inside of the basket, pinning it securely before you sew.

## Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** Use the tapestry needle to tuck in any loose ends of yarn. Insert your needle under the stitches on the inside of the basket and pull it through for a neat finish. 
- **Blocking (optional):** This is when you shape and set your crochet piece by lightly steaming it and laying it flat to dry. It helps hold the form of the finished basket.
