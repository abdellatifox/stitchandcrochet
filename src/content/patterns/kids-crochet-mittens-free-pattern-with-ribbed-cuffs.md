### Introduction
In this tutorial, we'll create adorable kids' crochet mittens with cozy ribbed cuffs. This pattern is easy and perfect for beginners. You'll be making warm mittens for little hands to enjoy during chilly weather!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:**  
  - Worsted weight (#4) yarn, 100% acrylic  
  - Color 1: Dark Blue (Approx. 120 yards)  
  - Color 2: Light Blue (Approx. 60 yards)  
  - Note: Worsted weight yarn is medium-thickness, making it easy to see your stitches while you work.

- **Crochet Hook:**  
  - 5 mm (H-8) — This size is perfect for creating tight, warm mittens.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):**  
  - These are small tools you can use to mark the start of your rounds or rows, so you don’t lose your place.

- **Scissors:**  
  - You’ll need these to cut your yarn at the end of your project.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:**  
  - Used for weaving in the ends of your yarn, making your mittens look neat.

- **Pins:**  
  - Optional tools for securing seams, especially handy when assembling pieces.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (ch):**  
  - This creates the foundation of your work. Make a loop with your yarn and pull the yarn through the loop to create small loops.

- **Single Crochet (sc):**  
  - Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over (wrap yarn around your hook), pull through, yarn over again, and pull through both loops on your hook. This creates a tight stitch.

- **Slip Stitch (sl st):**  
  - Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, and pull the yarn straight through the stitch and also through the loop on your hook. This will help you join pieces.

- **Increase (inc):**  
  - This means you will add 2 single crochets in the same stitch, making your work wider.

- **Decrease (dec):**  
  - This means you will work two single crochets together to reduce the stitch count. Insert your hook in the first stitch, yarn over, pull through, then insert your hook in the next stitch, yarn over, pull through, yarn over again, and pull through all three loops.

## Pattern Details
- **Skill Level:** Beginner
- **Finished Size:** Fits children ages 4 to 8 years, approximately 6.5 inches (16.5 cm) from cuff to tip.
- **Time to Complete:** Approximately 3-4 hours.
- **Yarn Amounts:**
  - Dark Blue: Approx. 120 yards
  - Light Blue: Approx. 60 yards
- **Terms:** This pattern uses US crochet terms.

## Abbreviations
- ch = chain
- sc = single crochet
- st = stitch
- sl st = slip stitch
- inc = increase (2 sc in the same st)
- dec = decrease (single crochet two stitches together)

## Special Stitches
There are no unusual stitches in this pattern, but be sure to get comfy with basic stitches like chain and single crochet!

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Mittens (Make 2)

#### Cuff:
1. With Light Blue yarn, **ch 10**.  
2. **Row 1:** Starting in the **second ch from the hook**, **sc** in each ch across. (9 stitches)
3. **Row 2:** **Ch 1**, turn. **sc** in each st across. (9 stitches)
4. **Repeat Row 2** until the cuff measures approximately **4 inches (10 cm)** long.

#### Join Cuff:
5. With the last row of the ribbed cuff facing, **sl st** the ends together to form a loop. Turn cuff inside out for a clean seam.

#### Mittens Body:
6. **Switch to Dark Blue yarn.**
7. **Round 1:** **Ch 1**, work **24 sc** evenly around the cuff. Place a stitch marker to mark the beginning of the round. (24 stitches)
8. **Round 2:** **Inc** in the first st, **sc** in the next 11 sts, **inc** in the next st, **sc** in the next 11 sts. (26 stitches)
9. **Round 3:** **sc** in each st around. (26 stitches)
10. **Round 4:** **Inc** in the first st, **sc** in the next 12 sts, **inc** in the next st, **sc** in the next 12 sts. (28 stitches)
11. **Rounds 5-10:** **sc** in each st around. (28 stitches each round)
12. **Round 11:** **Sc**, **dec** across the round. (14 stitches)
13. **Round 12:** To shape the fingers, **sc** in the first 7 sts, **leave remaining sts unworked.**
14. **Round 13:** **Ch 1**, turn, **sc** in each of the 7 sts. (7 stitches)
15. **Rounds 14-15:** Repeat Round 13. (7 stitches)
16. **Fasten off**, leaving a long tail for sewing.

#### Finish Thumb:
17. **Reattach Dark Blue yarn** in the opening where the thumb hole was.
18. **Round 1:** **Sc** in the first 2 unworked sts from the previous round, **then sc** in the chain 1 made for the thumb. (3 stitches)
19. **Rounds 2-4:** **Sc** in each st around.
20. **Fasten off**, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
- Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle to tidy them up.
- You can optionally add small pom-poms or embellishments to the top of the mittens for added decoration.

## Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** Use your tapestry needle to thread the ends of yarn into the fabric of your mitten to hide them. This will keep your mittens looking neat and polished.
- **Blocking (optional):** This involves gently steaming or wetting your mittens and reshaping them if needed. It can help even out stitches and maintain your mitten shape, but is not necessary for this project.
