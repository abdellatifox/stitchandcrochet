Welcome! Today, we’re going to crochet a lovely pouf ottoman cover. It’s super stylish and functional, perfect for extra seating or as a cozy footrest. Don’t worry if you’re new to crochet; this pattern is beginner-friendly!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:**
  - Bulky (weight category 6) — this medium-thick yarn is easy to work with and makes a sturdy pouf.
  - Approximate amount: 
    - Color A (Charcoal Gray) - 3 skeins (about 200 yards each)
    - Color B (Cream) - 1 skein (about 200 yards)
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - 9 mm hook (US N/P-15) — the larger hook makes it easy to work with bulky yarn.
- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** 
  - These help keep track of rounds, especially if you're new to crochet.
- **Scissors:** 
  - For cutting your yarn.
- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** 
  - Used to weave in the ends and sew pieces together.
- **Stuffing:** 
  - Polyester stuffing or bean bag filling (about 2 pounds) gives your pouf its shape.
- **Fabric for Lining (optional):** 
  - This supports the pouf if you want to add extra structure.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (ch):** This is a basic stitch that creates a series of loops to start your work. You do this by pulling the yarn through the loop on your hook.
- **Double Crochet (dc):** This stitch gives your work height. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook).
  2. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over again and pull through the stitch (you now have three loops on your hook).
  4. Yarn over and pull through the first two loops (you’ll have two loops left).
  5. Yarn over again and pull through the last two loops.
- **Slip Stitch (sl st):** This is used to join rounds or pieces together.
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull through the stitch and through the loop on your hook at the same time.
- **Single Crochet (sc):** A more compact stitch than double crochet. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull through (you should have two loops on your hook).
  3. Yarn over again and pull through both loops on your hook.
- **Single Crochet Two Together (sc2tog):** This reduces the stitch count by crocheting two stitches together as one:
  1. Insert hook into the first stitch and yarn over.
  2. Pull through (you have two loops on your hook).
  3. Insert your hook into the next stitch and yarn over again.
  4. Pull through (you now have three loops on your hook).
  5. Yarn over and pull through all three loops.

## Pattern Details
- **Skill Level:** Beginner
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 20 inches (51 cm) in diameter and 12 inches (30 cm) in height
- **Time to Complete:** About 4 hours total
- **Yarn Amounts:**
  - Color A (Charcoal Gray) - 3 skeins
  - Color B (Cream) - 1 skein
- **Terms:** This pattern uses US crochet terms. The difference in UK terms is mainly that double crochet in the US is equivalent to treble crochet in the UK.

## Abbreviations
- ch = chain
- dc = double crochet
- sl st = slip stitch
- st = stitch
- sp = space
- rnd = round

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Base
1. **Ch 4**, join with a sl st to form a ring. (1 st)
2. **Rnd 1:** Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), then make 11 dc in the ring. Join with a sl st to the top of the ch 3. (12 sts)
3. **Rnd 2:** Ch 3, make 1 dc in the same st, then *make 2 dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Rnd 3:** Ch 3, make 1 dc in the same st, then *make 1 dc in the next st, 2 dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (36 sts)
5. **Rnd 4:** Ch 3, make 1 dc in the same st, then *make 1 dc in the next 2 sts, 2 dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (48 sts)
6. **Rnd 5:** Ch 3, make 1 dc in the same st, then *make 1 dc in the next 3 sts, 2 dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (60 sts)
7. **Rnd 6:** Ch 3, make 1 dc in the same st, then *make 1 dc in the next 4 sts, 2 dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (72 sts)

### Side
8. **Rnd 7:** Ch 3, make 1 dc in the same st, then dc in each st around. Join with a sl st. (72 sts)
9. **Rnd 8-18:** Repeat round 7 for a total of 11 rounds (This means you will have a total of 12 rounds for the side). You should have a height of about 12 inches (30 cm). 

### Top
10. **Rnd 19:** Ch 1, make 1 sc in the same st, then sc in each st around. Join with a sl st. (72 sts)
11. **Rnd 20:** Ch 1, then *make sc2tog in the next 2 sts*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (36 sts)
12. **Rnd 21:** Ch 1, then *make sc2tog*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (18 sts)
13. **Rnd 22:** Ch 1, then *make sc2tog*. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st. (9 sts)
14. **Fasten off** and leave a long tail for sewing.

### Assembly / Putting It All Together
- Turn the pouf cover inside out. 
- Stuff it with polyester filling until it feels firm but comfortable. 
- Use the long tail from round 22 to sew the top closed using a whip stitch. 
- Weave in any remaining ends.
- Optional: For added structure, you can line the inside with a fabric before stuffing it.

### Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** To do this, use your yarn/tapestry needle to thread the yarn tail back and forth through the stitches. This secures it so it won’t unravel.
- **Blocking (optional):** This is the process of wetting your finished item and shaping it to dry. It helps even out stitches and create a nice shape. If your pouf looks perfect, you can skip this step!
