## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - **Worsted weight yarn (Category 4)** — This is a medium-thickness yarn that is easy to see while you crochet.
  - **Colors:** 
    - Main Color: Charcoal Gray (approx. 200 yards)
    - Contrast Color: Soft White (approx. 50 yards)
  
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - **5.5 mm (I-9)** crochet hook — This size is great for working with worsted weight yarn.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** These can help you keep track of your stitches if you're unsure where to start a new row.

- **Scissors:** You'll need these to cut your yarn at the end.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** This needle is used for weaving in the loose ends of your yarn when you finish.

- **Measuring Tape:** Helpful for making adjustments if necessary.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (CH):** This creates the initial loop. To make a chain, wrap the yarn over the hook and pull through the loop on your hook.
  
- **Half Double Crochet (HDC):** 
  1. Yarn over (yarn goes over the hook).
  2. Insert the hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over again.
  4. Pull this yarn through the stitch (you now have 3 loops on your hook).
  5. Yarn over one last time and pull through all 3 loops.

- **Single Crochet (SC):** 
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull through (you have 2 loops on your hook).
  3. Yarn over again and pull through both loops.

- **Slip Stitch (SL ST):** 
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull through both loops on your hook. This creates a tight connection that can join pieces.

## Pattern Details
- **Skill Level:** Beginner

- **Terms:** This pattern uses US crochet terms. The main difference with UK terms is a "double crochet" in the US is equivalent to a "treble crochet" in the UK.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Body of the Sock
#### Round 1
1. **Foundation Row:** CH 25. 
   - *This creates the base length of your sock.*

#### Round 2
2. **Row 1:** HDC in the 2nd CH from hook and in each CH across.  
   - You’ll have 24 HDC stitches now. 

#### Round 3
3. **Row 2:** CH 1, turn. HDC in each stitch across.   
   - You've done this for now 2 rows.

#### Round 4
4. **Row 3 to Row 10:** Continue to CH 1, turn. HDC in each st across for 9 rows. 
   - You'll have completed 10 rows total when you finish.

#### Round 5
5. **Row 11:** Change to Contrast Color. CH 1, turn. HDC in each stitch across. 
   - You’ll still have 24 HDC stitches.

#### Round 6
6. **Row 12-13:** CH 1, turn. HDC in each stitch across using Main Color for these two rows. 
   - You’ll have 24 HDC both times.

### Heel Flap
#### Round 7
7. **Row 14:** HDC in the first 12 HDC, CH 1, turn.  
   - This means you only work into the first half of your sock now.

#### Round 8
8. **Row 15:** HDC in each stitch across for this row. CH 1, turn.  
   - You still have 12 HDC stitches. 

#### Round 9
9. **Row 16:** Repeat Row 15 until there are 6 rows total.  
   - This makes the heel nice and sturdy.

### Closure
#### Round 10
10. **Row 22:** Fold in half lengthwise with the WS (wrong side) facing out. SL ST through both layers along the side edges to close the heel section.  
   - You’re creating the closed shape for the heel with 12 working stitches.

#### Round 11
11. **Row 23:** CH 1, HDC down the side around the sock opening. Join with a SL ST at the beginning.  
   - You’ll have 24 HDC for the opening now.

### Cuff
#### Round 12
12. **Row 24:** CH 1, HDC in the back loop of each stitch around. Join with a SL ST.  
   - You should still have 24 HDC.

#### Round 13
13. **Row 25-28:** Repeat Row 24 four more times for a stretchy cuff.  
   - This adds a nice finishing touch to your sock!

### Finishing
#### Round 14
14. **Weave in Ends:** Using a tapestry needle, weave in all loose ends. 
   - This tidies up your project.

#### Round 15
15. **Block the Slipper Socks:** Lightly steam or wet block to shape as desired. 
   - This is optional but can help your socks look fantastic!

## Measurements & Dimensions
- **Final dimensions of the finished item:** 
  - Length: 10 inches (25.4 cm)
  - Width: 4.5 inches (11.4 cm)
- **Measurements for Men’s Size:** Increase the initial chain to 30 for a larger fit.

## Timing Information
- **Estimated working time:**
  - Body: 1 hour
  - Heel Flap: 30 minutes
  - Cuff: 30 minutes
  - Finishing: 30 minutes
  - **Total time:** Approximately 2.5 hours

## Variations & Notes
- For a lighter sock, consider using cotton yarn.
- Ensure a snug fit around the arch for comfort.
- Always wash your slippers in cold water and dry them flat to maintain shape.
