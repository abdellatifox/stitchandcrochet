## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - Worsted Weight (Category 4) — This is a medium-thickness yarn that's easy to see your stitches with.
  - **Main Color (MC):** Soft Grey (approx. 450 yards / 410 meters)
  - **Contrast Color (CC):** Cream (approx. 150 yards / 140 meters)

- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - 5.5 mm (I-9) crochet hook — This is the size you'll use to make your stitches.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** These help keep track of your rounds or rows.

- **Scissors:** You'll use these to cut your yarn.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** This is for weaving in your ends after you finish crocheting.

- **Stuffing (if applicable):** Polyester Fiberfill for stuffing, about 1 bag (8 oz).

- **Safety Eyes (if applicable):** Not used in this pattern, but good to know for other projects!

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (ch):** Create a series of loops to start your work. For example, if the pattern says to chain 60, make a total of 60 loops.
- **Double Crochet (dc):** This stitch is taller than a single crochet. Begin by yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook). Insert the hook into the stitch, yarn over again, pull up a loop (you will have 3 loops on the hook). Yarn over and pull through the first 2 loops, yarn over again and pull through the last 2 loops. 
- **Slip Stitch (sl st):** This is used to join rounds or pieces together. Insert your hook, yarn over, and pull the yarn through both the stitch and the loop on your hook.
- **Single Crochet (sc):** Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop (2 loops on the hook). Yarn over again and pull through both loops.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Body
#### Foundation Chain
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 60. (60 sts)

#### Row 1
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. Turn. (58 dc)

#### Row 2
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each dc across. Turn. (58 dc)

#### Rows 3-19
4. **Row 3-19:** Repeat Row 2 (58 dc).

### Shaping the Arm Nest
#### Row 20
5. **Row 20:** Ch 1, sc in first dc, *ch 2, skip next 2 dc, sc in next dc* repeat from * to * across. Do not turn. (End with a sc in the last dc; you will be working in rounds now).

#### Round 1
6. **Round 1:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in first sc, *ch 2, skip next 2 ch, dc in next sc* repeat from * to * around. Join to top of beginning ch-3 with sl st. (58 dc and 29 ch-2 spaces)

#### Rounds 2-5
7. **Round 2-5:** Ch 3, dc in each ch-2 space (remember to treat ch-3 as a dc); join with sl st. Continue until the piece measures approximately 12 inches high from the start of the ch.

### Edging
#### Sc Edging
8. **Sc Edging:** Ch 1, sc evenly around the entire piece, placing 3 sc in each corner. Join with sl st to the first sc.

#### Buttonhole (optional)
9. **Buttonhole:** Ch 1, sc in first st, ch 2, skip a few stitches based on your button size (about 2 stitches), sc in the next st. Finish around to ensure the button fits snugly into the buttonhole.

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
1. Weave in all ends with a tapestry needle. 
2. Stuff the Arm Nest with polyester fiberfill to your desired firmness. 
3. If needed, block the object to help maintain its shape.

## Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** Use your tapestry needle to sew in any loose ends from your yarn. This helps to tidy up your piece.
- **Blocking (optional):** Blocking involves dampening your finished piece and laying it flat to dry to help it keep its shape and look its best.
