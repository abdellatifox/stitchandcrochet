## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:**
  - **Bulky weight yarn, 100% acrylic**  
    You'll need about 600 yards (550 meters) of Soft Gray and 200 yards (180 meters) of Cream. Bulky yarn is perfect because it's thick, making it easier to see your stitches. It also works up quickly.
  
- **Crochet Hook:**  
  - **10 mm (N/P-15)**  
    This large hook size helps create the nice, thick texture of the rug.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):**  
These markers can help you keep track of your rounds, ensuring you don’t lose your place.

- **Scissors:**  
To cut your yarn when you're finished.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:**  
Used for weaving in ends. This helps give your project a neat finish.

- **Fabric or rubber rug grip backing (optional, for non-slip):**  
This backing can be attached to the bottom of your rug to help it stay in place.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (ch):**  
  To make a foundation for your work, you'll create small loops. 

- **Single Crochet (sc):**  
  To create the main fabric of your rug. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook).
  3. Pull the yarn through the stitch (you will have two loops on your hook).
  4. Yarn over again.
  5. Pull through the two loops on your hook. You've completed a single crochet!

- **Slip Stitch (sl st):**  
  This stitch is used to join rounds together. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over.
  3. Pull the yarn directly through the stitch and the loop on your hook.

- **Increase (inc):**  
  This means adding two single crochet stitches into the same stitch.

- **Decrease (dec):**  
  This means stitching two single crochet stitches together into one stitch.

## Abbreviations
- ch = chain
- sc = single crochet
- sl st = slip stitch
- st(s) = stitch(es)
- inc = increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec = decrease (2 sc worked together as one)

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Round 1: Foundation
1. **Ch 4**: Create a chain of 4 stitches and join with a slip stitch to form a ring. (1 st)
2. **Ch 1**, then **8 sc into the ring**: Insert your hook into the middle of the ring. Yarn over and pull through. You now have 2 loops on your hook. Yarn over again and pull through both loops on your hook to make your first single crochet. Repeat until you have 8 single crochet stitches in the ring. Join with a slip stitch to the first single crochet. (8 sts)

### Round 2: Increase
3. **Ch 1**, then **2 sc in each st around**: Make sure to place your hook into each of the 8 stitches and do 2 single crochet stitches into each of them. Join with a slip stitch to the first single crochet. (16 sts)

### Round 3: Increase
4. **Ch 1**, then **[1 sc in next st, 2 sc in next st] repeat around**: This means you will do one single crochet in the first stitch, and then in the next stitch, do 2 single crochets. Repeat this until you have finished the round. Join with a slip stitch. (24 sts)

### Round 4: Increase
5. **Ch 1**, then **[1 sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st] repeat around**: Do one single crochet in each of the first 2 stitches, and then do 2 single crochets in the next one. Repeat until completed. Join with a slip stitch. (32 sts)

### Round 5: Increase
6. **Ch 1**, then **[1 sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st] repeat around**: Do a single crochet in each of the first 3 stitches, and then do 2 single crochets in the next. Repeat around. Join with a slip stitch. (40 sts)

### Round 6: Increase
7. **Ch 1**, then **[1 sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st] repeat around**: Do a single crochet in the first 4 stitches, and then do 2 single crochets in the next. Repeat until the end of the round. Join with a slip stitch. (48 sts)

### Rounds 7-12: Continue Round
8. **Ch 1**, then **1 sc in each st around**: For the next 6 rounds, simply make one single crochet in each of the 48 stitches. Join with a slip stitch at the end of each round. (48 sts)

### Rounds 13-14: Color Change to Cream
9. **Round 13:** Change to Cream yarn. **Ch 1**, then **1 sc in each st around**: Join with a slip stitch. (48 sts)
10. **Round 14:** **Ch 1**, then **1 sc in each st around**: Join with a slip stitch. (48 sts)

### Edging (Optional)
11. **Ch 1**, then *sc in first st, ch 1, skip next st, sc in next st* around: This creates a nice decorative edge. Join with a slip stitch.

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
- If you decided to use a rug grip backing, attach it to the underside of your rug using fabric glue or follow the manufacturer’s instructions.
- Take your tapestry needle and carefully weave in all of the yarn ends. This gives your rug a polished look.
- If your rug has any bumps or doesn’t lie flat, you can block it. Blocking is the process of dampening your crochet piece and then shaping it to dry flat.

## Finishing
### Weave in Ends
Using your yarn or tapestry needle, thread the end of the yarn and pull it through the stitches on the back of the rug. This locks the yarn in place and keeps your project looking neat. 

### Blocking (optional)
Blocking can help your rug lay flat. To block, moisten the rug gently (you can spray it with some water) and lay it out flat in the desired shape. Allow it to dry completely.
