Creating a sturdy crochet basket is a fun way to organize your space or add a stylish touch to your home decor. In this tutorial, I’ll walk you through making a beautiful crochet basket that will hold its shape perfectly, even when filled. Don’t worry if you’re new to this—I'll guide you every step of the way!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn**:  
   - Bulky (5) — this is a thicker yarn that makes the basket sturdy and easy to work with.  
   - Colors:  
     - Natural White (100 yards)  
     - Sandy Beige (100 yards)  

- **Stiffener**:  
   - Fabric stiffener solution (1 bottle, approx. 8 oz) — this helps your basket maintain its shape and provides firmness.

- **Other materials**:  
   - Craft wire or plastic canvas (1 piece, 10 inches × 10 inches) — this supports the base of your basket.  
   - Cotton thread in black (optional, 20 yards) — for decorative stitches.

## Tools & Equipment
- **Hook Size**:  
   - 8.0 mm (L) or US size L — this size is perfect for working with bulky yarn.

- **Other Tools**:  
   - Tapestry needle — used for weaving in ends.  
   - Stitch markers — helpful for marking your rounds.  
   - Scissors — for cutting yarn.  
   - Measuring tape — to ensure your basket is the right size.  
   - Blocking mats (optional) — to help shape your basket as it dries.  
   - Small brush for applying stiffener (if not using a spray).

## Gauge
- Gauge: 8 stitches and 6 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in single crochet (SC).  
- Make a swatch of 12 stitches and 10 rows to check your gauge. If your swatch doesn’t match, adjust your hook size.

## Abbreviations & Special Stitches
- SC: Single Crochet — a basic crochet stitch that is solid and tight.
- CH: Chain — creates a foundation for your work. 
- SL ST: Slip Stitch — a stitch used to join or move the yarn without adding height.
- STS: Stitches — the individual loops created by crochet stitches.
- FO: Finish Off — to cut the yarn and secure your work.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Base of the Basket:
1. **CH 4**, join with a SL ST to form a ring. (1 ST)
   - *Tip: Pull the yarn tail snug to secure your ring.*

2. **Round 1**: CH 1 (this does not count as a stitch), make **8 SC** into the ring, join with a SL ST. (8 STS)

3. **Round 2**: CH 1, make **2 SC in each SC around**, join with a SL ST. (16 STS)

4. **Round 3**: CH 1, *make 1 SC in next ST, then 2 SC in next ST*. Repeat from * to * around, join with a SL ST. (24 STS)

5. **Round 4**: CH 1, *make 1 SC in next 2 STS, then 2 SC in next ST*. Repeat from * to * around, join with a SL ST. (32 STS)

6. **Round 5**: CH 1, *make 1 SC in next 3 STS, then 2 SC in next ST*. Repeat from * to * around, join with a SL ST. (40 STS)

### Sides of the Basket:
7. **Round 6**: CH 1, make **1 SC in each ST around**, join with a SL ST. (40 STS)

8. **Rounds 7–15**: Repeat **Round 6**, making 1 SC in each ST around until the basket measures 8 inches (20 cm) tall.

### Finishing:
9. **Last Round**: CH 1, make **1 SL ST in each ST around**, join with a SL ST. (40 STS)

10. **FO (Finish Off)** your yarn and use a tapestry needle to weave in any remaining ends.

## Assembly & Finishing
1. **Base Support**: Cut the craft wire to fit the base of the basket. Shape it into a circle and insert it into the base of the basket. If using plastic canvas, trim it to fit and insert it in the same way.

2. **Stiffening**: Following the package instructions, mix the fabric stiffener. You can immerse the basket in the stiffener or use a brush to apply it directly onto the surface. Allow the basket to dry completely. This step is crucial for maintaining the basket's shape!

3. **Decorative Stitches** (optional): Use the black cotton thread to crochet a decorative edge along the top of the basket or add embellishments as you like.

## Measurements & Dimensions
- Final dimensions of the finished basket:  
   - Height: 8 inches (20 cm)  
   - Width: 10 inches (25 cm)  

## Timing Information
- Estimated time to complete the basket: 4-6 hours, depending on your speed.
  - Base: 1-2 hours  
  - Sides: 2-3 hours  
  - Finishing & stiffening: 1 hour  

## Variations & Notes
- For a smaller basket, you can start with a CH of 3 and adjust the rounds accordingly to reduce the circumference.
- Use multiple colors in rounds for a stylish striped effect.
- To ensure your basket is strong enough, make sure your stitches are tight. Loose stitches could cause the basket to weaken.

Remember to hand wash your finished basket with a mild detergent and avoid machine washing. Store the basket in a cool, dry place to maintain its shape.
