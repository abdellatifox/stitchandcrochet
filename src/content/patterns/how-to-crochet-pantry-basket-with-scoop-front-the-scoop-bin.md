You'll love making this crochet pantry basket with a scoop-shaped front! It's perfect for easily accessing your kitchen or craft supplies. Don’t worry if you’re new to crocheting; I’ll guide you through this simple project step by step.

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:**
  - Worsted weight (Medium; 4) — This is a medium-thickness yarn, which is easy to see and work with.
  - Colors:
    - Color A: "Natural" (approx. 200 yards/183 meters)
    - Color B: "Charcoal" (approx. 100 yards/91 meters)
  
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - 5.5 mm (US size I/9) — This hook size is just right for working with worsted weight yarn.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** 
  - These are used to mark important points in your work, like the beginning of rounds.

- **Scissors:** 
  - For cutting your yarn when you finish or need to change colors.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** 
  - This needle is used for weaving in loose ends and finishing off your work.

- **Fiberfill or Similar Stuffing (optional):** 
  - If you want to soften the edges of your basket.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
1. **Chain (ch):** 
   - To create a chain stitch, simply yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook) and pull through the loop on your hook. This stitch creates the foundation of your project.

2. **Single Crochet (sc):** 
   - Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop (you now have two loops on your hook), yarn over again and pull through both loops. This creates a solid fabric.

3. **Slip Stitch (sl st):** 
   - Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, and pull the yarn through both the stitch and the loop on your hook. This stitch is used to join or move to another part of your work without adding height.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Base
1. **Ch 20.**
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (19 sc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each sc across. (19 sc)
4. **Repeat Row 2 until the base measures 12” (30 cm) from the beginning. This will be approximately 10 rows total.** 

### Sides
5. **Row 11:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. (19 sc)
6. **Row 12:** Ch 1, turn; sc in first 9 sts, ch 5, skip next 5 sts, sc in last 5 sts. (19 sc + 5 ch)
7. **Row 13-20:** Ch 1, turn; sc in each st across. (19 sc)

*After Row 20, the height should measure approximately 8” (20 cm) from the base.*

### Scoop Front
8. **Row 21:** Ch 1, turn; sc in first 9 sts, ch 5, sc in the ch-5 space (this creates the scoop shape), sc in last 5 sts. (19 sc + 5 ch)
9. **Row 22-26:** Repeat Row 21. 

### Finishing Edging
10. **Make edge:** Ch 1 and sc evenly around the entire top edge of the basket. Ensure to sc around the scoop. Join with a sl st to the first sc.

*You can optionally stuff the edges with fiberfill for added shape and firmness at this point.*

## Assembly & Finishing
- Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle. This means threading the loose ends through the fabric to hide them.
- If desired, lightly block the basket by wetting it and shaping it into your desired form and drying it flat. This helps the basket keep its shape.
- The scoop front feature should allow easy access to pantry items or craft supplies.

## Measurements & Dimensions
- Final dimensions of the basket: approximately 12” wide x 8” tall x 8” deep (30 cm x 20 cm x 20 cm).

## Timing Information
- **Estimated working time:**
  - Base: 1.5 hours
  - Sides and scoop: 2 hours 
  - Finishing: 30 minutes 
  - **Total: Approximately 4 hours.**

## Variations & Notes
- **Color variations:** Feel free to use any colors you like for your pantry basket to match your kitchen decor.
- **Common mistakes to avoid:** Be careful not to skip any of the chain stitches when creating the scoop front. It’s crucial for forming the shape. Also, ensure you are crocheting tightly but comfortably to keep your basket sturdy.
- **Care instructions:** Hand wash or machine wash on a gentle cycle; lay flat to dry to maintain its shape.
