This chunky crochet throw blanket is a cozy and stylish addition to any home décor, perfect for snuggling up on chilly evenings. Designed specifically for beginners, it utilizes simple stitches and techniques for an easy, enjoyable crafting experience.

### WHY YOU’LL LOVE THIS PATTERN
This blanket is a fantastic project for beginners because it only uses one simple stitch, the half double crochet (HDC), which makes it easy to master. With just a bit of time, around 9 to 11 hours, you’ll create a lovely, warm throw that’s both practical and beautiful. Plus, you can choose to add a pop of color for extra fun!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:**  
  - Bulky (5) yarn, which is thick and plush, making it perfect for warm blankets. 
  - Main Color: Soft Gray (approx. 900 yards / 823 m)
  - Accent Color (optional): Cream (approx. 200 yards / 183 m)

- **Crochet Hook:**  
  - 10 mm (N/P) hook, which is thick enough to handle bulky yarn easily.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):**  
  - These help keep track of your rows, especially in long projects like blankets.

- **Scissors:**  
  - For cutting your yarn when you finish.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:**  
  - This is used for weaving in ends so they don’t unravel.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (ch):** Create a foundation of loops to start your project. To chain, make a slip knot on your hook first, then yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook) and pull through the loop on your hook. Repeat this for as many chains as needed.
  
- **Half Double Crochet (HDC):** This stitch creates a thicker fabric. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook).
  2. Insert the hook into the stitch where you want to work.
  3. Yarn over again and pull through. You’ll have three loops on the hook.
  4. Yarn over once more and pull through all three loops on the hook. You've created one half double crochet stitch!

- **Stitches (st(s)):** This term refers to the individual units of crochet work created by your stitches.

## Let’s Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions
### Main Body of the Blanket
1. **Foundation Row:** Chain 80. (79 stitches)
2. **Row 1:** In the 3rd chain from the hook, work 1 HDC. HDC in each chain across. Turn your work at the end of the row. (78 stitches)
3. **Row 2:** Chain 2 (this counts as your first HDC). HDC in each stitch across. Turn. (78 stitches)
4. **Repeat Row 2** until your blanket measures approximately 60 inches in length. You’ll need a total of 60 rows.

### Color Change (Optional)
If you want to add a pop of color, follow these steps:
1. **Row 61:** At the start of this row, change to your Accent Color (Cream). Chain 2 and work 1 HDC in each stitch across. Turn. (78 stitches)
2. **Row 62:** Chain 2 and HDC in each stitch across. Turn. (78 stitches)

### Finish
1. **Row 63:** Change back to Main Color (Soft Gray). Chain 2 and HDC in each stitch across. Turn. (78 stitches)
2. **Row 64:** Chain 2 and HDC in each stitch across. Turn. (78 stitches)
3. **Fasten off:** Cut your yarn, leaving a long tail for weaving in. Pull the tail through the last stitch to secure it.

**Beginner Tip:** It’s okay if the edges of your blanket are not perfectly straight; it adds to its charm!

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
- **Weaving in Ends:** Thread your tapestry needle with the yarn tail left from fastening off. Weave it through several stitches on the back of your blanket to hide it. Do this for all loose ends until they are secure.

- **Optional Edging:** If you want a decorative touch, you can add an optional single crochet border:
  1. Start anywhere on the edge of your blanket. Work 1 HDC around the entire blanket, putting 1 HDC in each side row and placing 3 HDC in each corner to keep the corners smooth and rounded.

- **Blocking:** This is a technique used to shape your blanket. Lay it flat on a surface, gently stretching it to the desired size and pinning it down. Allow it to dry in this position.

## Finishing
### Weave in Ends
Using your tapestry needle, pull your yarn tail through several stitches on the back of your project to hide it. Cut any excess yarn once it’s secured.

### Blocking (Optional)
You don’t have to block your blanket, but doing so can help it look neater. Lay the blanket flat and stretch it gently if needed. Pin it down to hold its shape. Allow it to dry completely.

### Full Numbered Instructions
1. Chain 80. (79 sts)  
2. HDC in the 3rd chain from hook and in each chain across. Turn. (78 sts)  
3. Ch 2, HDC in each st across. Turn. (78 sts)  
4. Repeat Row 2 until blanket is 60 inches long (approximately 60 rows).  
5. Change to Accent Color: Ch 2, HDC in each st across. Turn. (78 sts)  
6. Ch 2, HDC in each st across. Turn. (78 sts)  
7. Change back to Main Color: Ch 2, HDC in each st across. Turn. (78 sts)  
8. Ch 2, HDC in each st across. Turn. (78 sts)  
9. Fasten off and weave in ends.  
10. Optional: Work HDC around the entire blanket for a border.
