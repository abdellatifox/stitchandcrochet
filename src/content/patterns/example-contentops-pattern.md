## Before you start

Work loosely so the ribbing stays stretchy. Chain 1 at the start of every row; it does not count as a stitch.

## Instructions

1. Ch 13.
2. **Row 1:** Sc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (12 sc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, sc BLO in each st across. Turn.
4. Repeat Row 2 until the band fits snugly around the head.

## Finishing

Hold the short ends together and slip stitch through both layers to join. Weave in the ends and turn right side out.

## FAQ

### Can I make it wider?

Yes — add chains in multiples of 1 at the start to make the band as wide as you like.
