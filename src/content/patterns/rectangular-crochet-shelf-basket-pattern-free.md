This rectangular crochet shelf basket free pattern makes practical and stylish storage solutions for your home. It's easy and satisfying to make, using basic double crochet stitches. You can create baskets in three sizes, perfect for organizing various items around the house.

Whether you're looking to tidy up your shelf or add a decorative touch to your space, this pattern is great for you. You'll learn how to work simple stitches and assemble them into functional baskets.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is beginner-friendly. You only need to know how to chain and double crochet. You'll also learn how to assemble the baskets at the end.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- **Small Basket:** 10 × 15 cm (4 × 6 in) 
- **Medium Basket:** 12 × 20 cm (5 × 8 in)
- **Large Basket:** 14 × 25 cm (6 × 10 in)
- **Gauge:** 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To measure gauge, make a swatch of 20 dc for 10 rows. Adjust your hook size if needed to get the correct gauge.

## Step 1: Small Basket (10 × 15 cm)
The small basket is worked flat for the bottom and sides.
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 33 with Color A.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across (31 dc).  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 2, turn; dc in each st across (31 dc).  
4. **Row 3–11:** Repeat Row 2 (31 dc each row).  
5. **Finish:** Fasten off.

## Step 2: Medium Basket (12 × 20 cm)
Next, we will make the medium basket.
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 37 with Color B.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across (35 dc).  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 2, turn; dc in each st across (35 dc).  
4. **Row 3–14:** Repeat Row 2 (35 dc each row).  
5. **Finish:** Fasten off.

## Step 3: Large Basket (14 × 25 cm)
Now let's create the large basket.
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 41 with Color C.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across (39 dc).  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 2, turn; dc in each st across (39 dc).  
4. **Row 3–16:** Repeat Row 2 (39 dc each row).  
5. **Finish:** Fasten off.

## How to Join Your Baskets
For each basket, follow these steps to join the sides.
- Fold the bottom up to create the basket sides.  
- Use sl sts to join each end of the basket together.  
- Fasten off and weave in all ends with a tapestry needle.  
- If needed, block the baskets to make sure the corners look sharp.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Basket
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your turns and finished rows.
- Count your stitches after each row to avoid mistakes.
- Check your tension, and adjust your hook size if it feels too loose or tight.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My sides look uneven." – You might be missing stitches; count them as you go to stay on track.
- "It’s too loose." – Try a smaller hook to tighten up your stitches.
- "The basket isn’t holding its shape." – Ensure you’re folding and joining straight. Block the basket for a sharper look.

## How to Care for Your Baskets
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid using bleach to keep the colors vibrant.

## FAQ
### How can I customize the size?
You can adjust the chain length in the foundation row and repeat the rows as needed for different heights.

### What type of yarn works best?
100% cotton yarn is recommended for durability and easy washing. You can use other yarns but ensure they have similar weight.

### How long will it take to make all three?
Approximately 6 hours for all three sizes; the small one takes about 1.5 hours, medium about 2 hours, and large about 2.5 hours.
