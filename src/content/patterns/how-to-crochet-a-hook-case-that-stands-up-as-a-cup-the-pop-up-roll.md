Welcome! Today, I'll walk you through making a unique crochet hook case that not only stores your hooks but also stands up as a cute cup. It’s functional and stylish, perfect for any crocheter. Let’s dive in!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - **Worsted weight cotton yarn** (Category 4) — This medium-thickness yarn is easy to work with and helps you see your stitches clearly.
  - **Colors:**
    - **Main Color:** Aqua (approx. 150 yards)
    - **Contrast Color:** Charcoal Gray (approx. 50 yards)
  
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - **5.0 mm (H-8)** crochet hook — This size is comfortable to hold for your stitches.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** 
  - They help you keep track of your rows and stitches.

- **Plastic Canvas:** 
  - About **5 x 5 inches** to add structure to your base.

- **Scissors:** 
  - For cutting your yarn.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** 
  - This is used for weaving in the ends of your project, ensuring it looks neat.

- **Fabric Stiffener (optional):** 
  - To make the case more sturdy if desired.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **ch (chain):** Create a foundation for your project by making loops. Hold the yarn and pull it through the loop on your hook.
- **dc (double crochet):** Yarn over the hook, insert it into the stitch, yarn over again and pull through, yarn over and pull through two loops, yarn over and pull through the last two loops.
- **sl st (slip stitch):** Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, and pull through both the stitch and the loop on your hook in one motion.
- **sc (single crochet):** Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull through, yarn over again and pull through both loops on your hook.
- **inc (increase):** Make two stitches in the same stitch. 
- **dec (decrease):** Crochet two stitches together as one.

## Pattern Details
- **Skill Level:** Intermediate
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 6 inches tall (15 cm) and 4 inches (10 cm) in diameter at the base when rolled up and standing.
- **Time to Complete:** Approximately 5 hours.
- **Yarn Amounts:** 
  - Light Aqua (Main Color): approx. 150 yards 
  - Dark Charcoal Gray (Contrast Color): approx. 50 yards.
- **Terms:** This pattern uses US crochet terms.

## Abbreviations
- **ch:** chain
- **dc:** double crochet
- **st(s):** stitch(es)
- **sl st:** slip stitch
- **inc:** increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)
- **dec:** decrease (crocheting 2 stitches together)

## Special Stitches
No special stitches are used in this pattern, so just focus on the basic stitches.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Body
#### Round 1
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 42. (42 sts)

#### Row 1
2. **Row 1:** Dc in the 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (40 sts)
   - **Beginner Tip:** The 3 chains you skipped at the beginning count as your first double crochet.

#### Row 2
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each st across. Turn. (40 sts)

#### Row 3 to Row 12
4. **Row 3-12:** Repeat Row 2. (40 sts each row)

#### Row 13
5. **Row 13:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (40 sts)

#### Row 14
6. **Row 14:** Ch 1, sc in the first 20 sts, inc in the next st, sc in the last 19 sts. Turn. (41 sts)
   - This means you will have one extra stitch in this row.

#### Row 15 to Row 18
7. **Row 15-18:** Repeat Row 2. (41 sts each row)

#### Row 19
8. **Row 19:** Ch 1, sc in the first 20 sts, dec in the next st, sc in the last 20 sts. (40 sts)

#### Row 20 to Row 22
9. **Row 20-22:** Repeat Row 2. (40 sts each row)

#### Row 23
10. **Row 23:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Fasten off. (40 sts)

### Base
#### Round 1
1. **Ch 5** and join to form a ring.

#### Round 1
2. **Round 1:** Ch 1, 8 sc in the ring. Join. (8 sts)

#### Round 2
3. **Round 2:** Ch 1, 2 sc in each st around. Join. (16 sts)

#### Round 3
4. **Round 3:** Ch 1, *sc in the next st, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (24 sts)

#### Round 4
5. **Round 4:** Ch 1, *sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (32 sts)

#### Round 5
6. **Round 5:** Ch 1, *sc in the next 3 sts, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (40 sts)

#### Round 6
7. **Round 6:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join. Fasten off. (40 sts)

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
1. Place the plastic canvas in the base of the crochet case.
2. Stitch around the edges with single crochet to secure it in place.
3. Shape the body into a cylinder. Sew or slip stitch the sides together using the final row of the body.
4. Weave in all ends securely using your yarn needle.
5. Optionally, apply fabric stiffener to give your Pop-Up Roll a sturdier structure.

## Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** Use the tapestry needle to thread any loose ends into the stitches of the crochet project so that everything looks clean and finished.
- **Blocking (optional):** This involves wetting your project and pinning it into shape to ensure it dries correctly. This is not always necessary for this project, but it can help if your stitches feel uneven.
