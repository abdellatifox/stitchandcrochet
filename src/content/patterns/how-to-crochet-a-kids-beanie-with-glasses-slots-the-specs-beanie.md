Welcome to your next crochet project! Today, we’re creating a charming and functional Specs Beanie that comes with special slots for glasses. This is perfect for little ones aged 4-10 who want to stay cozy while sporting their eyewear. Don't worry; this pattern is friendly for beginners!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - Worsted weight (category 4) yarn: This is a medium thickness yarn that is easy to see as you crochet. 
  - Choose the following colors:
    - **Main Color (MC)**: Sky Blue (approx. 150 yards)
    - **Contrast Color (CC)**: Dark Gray (approx. 50 yards)

- **Crochet Hook:** 5.5 mm (I-9, US size) crochet hook—this is the standard size for worsted weight yarn.

- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** These help keep track of rounds as you crochet, making your work easier.

- **Scissors:** To cut your yarn when you finish.

- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** A large needle used to weave in any loose ends of yarn so your project looks neat.

- **Measuring Tape:** For sizing the beanie and ensuring the correct fit.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **Chain (ch):** This creates the little loops that start your work. To make a chain, simply wrap the yarn around your hook and pull it through the loop already on the hook. Repeat for the number of chains needed.
  
- **Double Crochet (dc):** This stitch creates height in your work. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Yarn over (wrap the yarn around your hook).
  2. Insert the hook into the stitch you’re working into.
  3. Yarn over again and pull the yarn back through the stitch (you’ll have three loops on your hook).
  4. Yarn over once more and pull through the first two loops (two loops remain).
  5. Yarn over again and pull through the last two loops (you’re left with one loop on the hook).

- **Slip Stitch (sl st):** This is a joining stitch. To make a slip stitch:
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull the yarn through the stitch and the loop on your hook at the same time.

- **Single Crochet (sc):** This stitch is like a mini double crochet. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull the yarn through (you’ll have two loops on your hook).
  3. Yarn over again and pull through both loops.

## Pattern Details
- **Skill Level:** Beginner
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 18 inches in circumference and 8 inches in height.
- **Time to Complete:** About 2-3 hours.
- **Yarn Amounts:** 
  - Main Color: 150 yards
  - Contrast Color: 50 yards
- **Terms:** This pattern uses US crochet terms.

## Abbreviations
- ch = chain
- dc = double crochet
- sl st = slip stitch
- st(s) = stitch(es)
- sp = space

## Special Stitches
- **dc increase:** To do this, you will work 2 double crochets in the same stitch. This helps build width in your beanie.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Hat Body
#### Round 1
- Ch 4, sl st to the first ch to form a ring. (1 ring)

#### Round 2
- Ch 2 (this counts as the first dc), then 11 dc into the ring. Join with sl st to the top of the ch-2. (12 dc)

#### Round 3
- Ch 2, *dc increase in the next st, dc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. Join with sl st. (18 dc)

#### Round 4
- Ch 2, *dc increase in the next st, 2 dc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. Join with sl st. (24 dc)

#### Round 5
- Ch 2, *dc increase in the next st, 3 dc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. Join with sl st. (30 dc)

#### Rounds 6-10
- Ch 2, dc in each st around. Join with sl st. (30 dc)

#### Round 11
- Ch 2, *dc in the next 3 sts, ch 2 for glasses slot, skip the next 2 sts,* repeat from * to * around. Join with sl st. (6 ch-2 spaces)

#### Rounds 12-15
- Ch 2, dc in each st and in each ch-2 space. Join with sl st. (30 dc)

#### Final Round
- Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (30 sc)

### Beginner Tip: Remember, the chain-2 spaces in Round 11 are crucial for creating the slots for glasses. Keep your spacing consistent!

### Assembly / Putting It All Together
- Weave in all loose ends using a tapestry needle. Pull the needle through yarn tails into the inside of the beanie to hide them. 
- If needed, block the beanie by gently stretching it to shape or using a steam iron to make it look neat.

## Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** Use your tapestry needle to tuck in any loose yarn ends neatly into the inside of the beanie, making sure they don’t show.
  
- **Blocking (optional):** This is when you reshape your hat after making it. You can lightly dampen it and lay it flat to dry in the finished shape. This helps maintain a nice form.

- **Materials:**
  - Worsted weight yarn, MC: 150 yards, CC: 50 yards
- **Tools:**
  - 5.5 mm crochet hook, scissors, tapestry needle, stitch markers (optional), measuring tape
- **Instructions:**
  1. Ch 4, sl st to form a ring. (1 ring)
  2. Ch 2, 11 dc into ring, sl st to top of ch-2. (12 dc)
  3. **Round 3:** Ch 2, *dc increase, dc*, repeat around. (18 dc)
  4. **Round 4:** Ch 2, *dc increase, 2 dc*, repeat around. (24 dc)
  5. **Round 5:** Ch 2, *dc increase, 3 dc*, repeat around. (30 dc)
  6. **Rounds 6-10:** Ch 2, dc in each st. (30 dc)
  7. **Round 11:** Ch 2, *dc in next 3, ch 2, skip 2*, repeat around. (6 ch-2 spaces)
  8. **Rounds 12-15:** Ch 2, dc in each st and ch-2 space. (30 dc)
  9. **Final Round:** Ch 1, sc in each st. (30 sc)
- **Notes:** Adjust size by checking gauge, and remember to weave in ends for a neat finish.
