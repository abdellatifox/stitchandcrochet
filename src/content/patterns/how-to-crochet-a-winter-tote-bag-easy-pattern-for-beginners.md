## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - Bulky weight (Weight 5) – this thick yarn works up quickly and keeps your bag warm and durable. You’ll need:
    - "Winter White" (300 yards)
    - "Charcoal Gray" (100 yards)
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - 9 mm (M/N) – this size helps make your stitches big and cozy.
- **Stitch Marker (optional but helpful):** 
  - This helps keep track of your rounds or rows as you work.
- **Scissors:** 
  - To cut your yarn when you finish.
- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** 
  - This needle is for weaving in the yarn ends when you are done.
- **Fabric Stiffener Spray (optional):** 
  - This helps the tote keep its shape and adds structure if desired.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **ch:** chain – a basic stitch that creates loops to begin your work. To make a chain stitch, simply wrap the yarn around your hook and pull it through the loop already on your hook.
  
- **hdc:** half double crochet – a stitch that gives your work some height. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Yarn over (yo), which means to wrap the yarn over your hook.
  2. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over again and pull through, so you have 3 loops on your hook.
  4. Yarn over again and pull through all 3 loops on your hook.

- **sl st:** slip stitch – a small stitch that joins pieces together without adding extra height.
  
- **sc:** single crochet – a tight stitch great for creating a sturdy fabric. Here’s how to make it:
  1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
  2. Yarn over and pull through (you will have 2 loops on your hook).
  3. Yarn over again and pull through both loops on your hook.

- **st(s):** stitch(es) – this is just a term for either one stitch or multiple stitches.

## Special Stitches
### Half Double Crochet (hdc)
1. Yarn over (yo), insert hook into the stitch.
2. Yarn over again and pull through (3 loops on your hook).
3. Yarn over again and pull through all 3 loops on your hook.

## Let's Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions
### Body of the Tote Bag
1. **Foundation:** Ch 36. 
2. **Row 1:** Hdc in the 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (35 hdc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Hdc in each st across. (35 hdc)
4. **Row 3-18:** Repeat Row 2 (35 hdc) for a total of 17 rows.

### Add Base
5. **Row 19:** Ch 1, turn. Hdc in the first 35 st, then work 12 hdc across the bottom into the foundation ch (make sure to work into the back bump for a clean edge), then hdc in the next 35 st along the other side. (82 hdc)
6. **Row 20:** Ch 1, turn. Hdc in each st around. (82 hdc)

### Create the Sides
7. **Row 21-26:** Repeat Row 20 (82 hdc) for a total of 6 rows.

### Join the Bag
8. **Row 27:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in the first 40 stitches, sl st in the next 2 stitches, sc in remaining 40 stitches. (82 sc). This creates an opening at the top.

### Straps
9. **Strap 1:** Ch 7.
10. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and across. Turn. (6 sc)
11. **Row 2-6:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across for 5 rows (6 sc).
12. **Fasten off**, leaving a long tail for sewing. 
13. **Repeat Steps 9-12 for Strap 2.**
14. **Attaching Straps:** Center each strap on the top edge of the tote, pin in place. Sew securely with the tapestry needle using the long tail from the strap.

## Assembly / Putting It All Together
1. Weave in all ends securely with your tapestry needle.
2. Optionally, block the tote bag to help shape it by laying it flat and pinning the edges.
3. If you want extra structure, spray with fabric stiffener and follow the instructions on the bottle.

## Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** Thread your needle with the yarn tail and go through a few stitches to secure it, then trim the excess yarn.
- **Blocking (optional):** This is the process of shaping your finished piece. It helps even out stitches and can improve the shape of your work. To block, wet the fabric, shape it as desired, and lay it flat to dry.
