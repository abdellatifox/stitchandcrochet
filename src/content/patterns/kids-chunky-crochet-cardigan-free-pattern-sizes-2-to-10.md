This cozy, chunky cardigan is perfect for kids aged 2 to 10. With an oversized fit and button-down front, it’s wonderful for layering. I’m excited to guide you through this beginner-friendly pattern!

## What You'll Need
- **Yarn:** 
  - **Bulky (5)** — This thick yarn is great for beginners and keeps your stitches visible. You will need approximately:
    - Main Color: Light Blue (400, 450, 500, 550, 600 yards for sizes 2, 4, 6, 8, 10)
    - Contrast Color: White for the button band (100 yards for all sizes)
- **Crochet Hook:** 
  - **9 mm (M/N) US** — This large hook works well with bulky yarn for quick projects.
- **Stitch Markers (optional but helpful):** 
  - These can help you keep track of your rounds and stitches, especially when starting new sections.
- **Scissors:** 
  - For cutting your yarn.
- **Yarn/Tapestry Needle:** 
  - Used for weaving in ends after finishing your project.
- **Measuring Tape:** 
  - To ensure your pieces are the correct sizes.
- **Pins (optional for assembly):** 
  - Can help keep pieces in place while you sew them together.
- **Buttons:** 
  - 3 buttons, 1" (25 mm) in diameter for the button band.

## Stitches You'll Use in This Pattern
- **ch:** (chain) 
  - To start your work, yarn over (wrap yarn over the hook) and pull through the loop on your hook.
- **sc:** (single crochet) 
  - Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop (2 loops on hook), yarn over again and pull through both loops.
- **dc:** (double crochet) 
  - Yarn over, insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop (3 loops on hook), yarn over and pull through the first 2 loops (2 loops remaining), yarn over and pull through the remaining 2 loops.
- **sl st:** (slip stitch) 
  - Insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop and then pull that loop through the loop on your hook.
- **st(s):** (stitch(es)) 
  - A general term for stitches.
- **sk:** (skip) 
  - To leave a stitch unworked.
- **inc:** (increase) 
  - Work 2 stitches into 1 stitch to make an extra stitch.

## Let’s Get Started: Full Pattern Instructions

### Body (Make 1 Piece)
- **Foundation Row:** 
  1. Ch 58 (for size 2; adjust accordingly for other sizes by adding or subtracting multiples of 2).

- **Row 1:** 
  1. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and across (57 sts). 
  2. Ch 1, turn.

- **Row 2:** 
  1. Sc in each st across (57 sts). 
  2. Ch 1, turn.

- **Rows 3-18:** 
  1. Repeat Row 2 for 16 more rows (57 sts).
  
- **Divide for Armholes:** 
  - **Row 19:** 
    1. Sc in 24 (for size 2) (26 for size 4, 28 for size 6, 30 for size 8, 32 for size 10) sts, 
    2. Ch 2, 
    3. Sk next 9 (for size 2 and 4) (11 for size 6 and 8), 
    4. Sc in remaining sts. 
    5. Ch 1, turn.

- **Rows 20-22:** 
  1. Sc in each st across (57 sts). 
  2. Ch 1, turn.

- **Row 23:** 
  1. Sc in 24 (for size 2) (26 for size 4, 28 for size 6, 30 for size 8, 32 for size 10) sts. 
  2. Ch 2 and turn.

- **Join Body at the Top:** 
  - **Row 24:** 
    1. With right side (RS) facing, sl st across to join armholes. 

### Sleeves (Make 2)
- **Foundation Row:** 
  1. Ch 22 (for size 2; adjust accordingly for other sizes).

- **Row 1:** 
  1. Sc in 2nd ch from hook and across (21 sts). 
  2. Ch 1, turn.

- **Row 2:** 
  1. Sc in each st across (21 sts). 
  2. Ch 1, turn.

- **Rows 3-12:** 
  1. Repeat Row 2 for 10 more rows (21 sts).

- **Shaping Sleeve:** 
  - **Row 13:** 
    1. Sc in 6 (for size 2) (7 for size 4, 8 for size 6, 9 for size 8, 10 for size 10) sts, 
    2. Inc (2 sc in next st), 
    3. Sc in remaining 5 (for size 2) (6 for size 4, 7 for size 6, 8 for size 8, 9 for size 10) sts. 
    4. Ch 1, turn.

- **Rows 14-20:** 
  1. Repeat Row 2 for 7 more rows.

- **Make Second Sleeve:** 
  1. Repeat all steps to create a second sleeve.

### Assembly / Putting It All Together
1. **Attach Sleeves:** 
   - Align sleeves to armholes. Sew using slip stitch or whip stitch.

2. **Sew Sides Together:** 
   - Attach front and back sides at the sides using single crochet.

3. **Button Band:** 
   - Using contrast color yarn, pick up and sc evenly along the front edge. 
   - Ch 1, turn.

4. **Create Buttonholes:** 
   - **Row 1:** 
     1. Sc to 3 sts before edge, 
     2. Ch 1, 
     3. Sk 1 st, 
     4. Sc in next. 
     5. Repeat until you have enough buttonholes evenly spaced for your buttons.

5. **Seam Up Open Edges:** 
   - Weave in all ends and tidy up any open edges.

6. **Wash and Block:** 
   - Gently wash and block the cardigan to shape it nicely.

### Finishing
- **Weave in Ends:** 
  - Using your yarn needle, thread the yarn tail and push it through several stitches on the inside of your project to secure it. Trim any excess yarn.

- **Blocking (optional):** 
  - This is a method of dampening and shaping your finished piece. Gently stretch and pin it to the desired size and shape to make it look nice.
