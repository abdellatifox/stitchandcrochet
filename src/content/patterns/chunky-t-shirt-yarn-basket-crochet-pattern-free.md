This chunky T-shirt yarn basket crochet pattern makes a sturdy and spacious basket with handles, ideal for storage or decor in your home. With simple stitches, you’ll find this project both easy and satisfying to create.

You'll learn how to work in rounds, increase stitches, and add handles. This basket is perfect for those seeking a practical yet stylish way to organize their space.

## Why You'll Love This Basket
- Quick to make – complete it in just 4–5 hours.
- Easy to customize – adjust the size or colors to fit your style.
- Sturdy construction – the T-shirt yarn gives it a solid feel.
- Useful for many purposes – great for storage, decor, or even as a gift.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches like single crochet and includes clear step-by-step instructions. You’ll pick up new skills as you go, such as working in rounds and adding handles.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st – stitch
- sts – stitches
- magic ring – a method to start working in the round: make a loop with the yarn, then work stitches into that loop.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approx. 30 cm (12 in) diameter and 25 cm (10 in) tall.
- Gauge: 5 sc and 5 rounds = 10 cm (4 in) square. Check gauge by making a small swatch. If your gauge does not match, adjust your hook size until it does.

## How to Crochet the Chunky T-Shirt Yarn Basket (Step by Step)

### Base
The base is worked in rounds starting from a magic ring. 
1. **Round 1:** Make a magic ring, ch 1, work 6 sc into the ring. Pull ring tight. (6 sts)
2. **Round 2:** 2 sc in each st around. (12 sts)
3. **Round 3:** *1 sc in the next st, 2 sc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. (18 sts)
4. **Round 4:** *1 sc in the next 2 sts, 2 sc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. (24 sts)
5. **Round 5:** *1 sc in the next 3 sts, 2 sc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. (30 sts)
6. **Round 6:** *1 sc in the next 4 sts, 2 sc in the next st,* repeat from * to * around. (36 sts)

### Sides
The sides are worked continuously in rounds.
7. **Round 7:** Work 1 sc in each st around. (36 sts)
8. **Rounds 8–18:** Repeat Round 7 for 11 more rounds. (total 12 rounds from the base, 36 sts)

   **At this point, the basket should measure approximately 10 inches tall.**
   
9. **Optional Stripe Round:** If using an accent color, switch to Midnight Black in Round 18. Change colors at the beginning of the round and work continuously for the next 2 rounds.

### Handles
Handles are created with chain stitches attached to the basket.
10. **Handle Placement:** Decide where you want to place your handles. Choose two opposite sides of the basket (one handle will be made at a time).
11. **Handle Round 1:** Skip 6 sts on each side for handle attachment, ch 1, (sc in next 12 sts, ch 6, skip 6 sts) twice. (Total ch gap of 12)
12. **Handle Round 2:** Working on the first chain, ch 1, sc in each ch across, sl st to the first sc of handle base to close. (6 sts back)
13. **Attach Yarn:** Fasten off and reattach yarn at the other handle’s chain, then repeat Round 2.

## Finishing
- **Join the Ends:** Weave in yarn ends using a tapestry needle to secure any edges.
- **Shape:** If desired, use fabric stiffener to help retain the shape.
- **Blocking:** Gently block the basket to encourage a uniform shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Basket
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches after each round to avoid mistakes.
- Keep your tension consistent for an even finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My basket is lopsided." – This may happen if you didn't keep track of your stitch count. Double-check your count and adjust if needed.
- "The handles are uneven." – This can occur if the handle placement isn't the same on both sides. Measure and adjust as needed.
- "The base is too small." – If your base feels small, check your increases. Make sure to follow the rounds correctly for the right size.

## How to Care for Your Basket
Hand wash gently and lay flat to dry. Do not wring out, as T-shirt yarn can lose its shape if handled roughly.

## FAQ
### How do I make the basket bigger?
To change the size, simply adjust the number of rounds in the sides until you reach your desired height.

### Can I use a different type of yarn?
Yes, you can use any bulky yarn. Just adjust your gauge and hook size accordingly.

### How can I keep the basket's shape?
Using fabric stiffener helps maintain the basket's shape after it’s made.
